# Spider-Man comic panels

Three original hand-authored SVG panels used by scene 4:

- `panel-swing.svg` — full-body web-swing above the city; classic black ink, red/blue suit blocks, print halftone, and a hand-set `THWIP!` sound effect.
- `panel-rooftop.svg` — masked hero crouching on a rooftop beneath a full moon.
- `panel-kiss.svg` — gentle upside-down kiss at sunset, based on the pose in the supplied reference images.

The assets follow the user's reference direction: bold printed-comic outlines, halftone, cream/mustard paper, and scarlet/cobalt colors. They're authored vector art rather than traced or embedded copies of the screenshots. Captions and alt text live in `index.html` so they remain accessible and editable.
